# Legal Review Memo for UAE Counsel

To: UAE-qualified legal counsel
From: Repository maintainers (almiccy-source)
Re: Contributor Covenant adoption and enforcement procedures in a UAE context

Background
- The repository adopted the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct. Recent
  updates added an enforcement contact (almiccy@gmail.com), a Reporting
  Privacy Notice summary, a detailed Reporting Privacy Notice, and a Moderator
  SOP. An Arabic summary translation was added.

Action requested
Please review the attached materials and advise on: legal risks, compliance
with UAE law (PDPL and cybercrime/defamation), recommended wording changes,
and an appropriate enforcement process that reduces exposure for the project
and maintainers.

Specific questions
1. Defamation / cybercrime risk
   - Does the current enforcement language or private investigation process
     expose the maintainers to criminal defamation or other cybercrime risk in
     the UAE? Advise on safe wording to avoid accidental criminal exposure.
2. Data protection (PDPL)
   - Are the processing purposes, lawful basis, retention (24 months), and
     cross-border transfer approach sufficient under PDPL? Are specific
     contractual clauses or safeguards required for GitHub and other processors?
3. Arabic translation
   - Is the Arabic summary legally sufficient for notice? Recommend wording or
     a certified translation requirement for legal enforceability.
4. Reporting contact
   - Using a personal Gmail address is currently listed (almiccy@gmail.com).
     Recommend a project-managed alias or other intake method for continuity and
     data control. Please advise on any legal or practical concerns with using
     a personal address.
5. Disclosure to law enforcement
   - Confirm the correct process for responding to UAE law enforcement
     requests for investigative materials; recommend any template language or
     verification steps.
6. Content removal and publication
   - Advise thresholds for public removal or naming respondents vs private
     corrective actions to manage legal risk.
7. Any other risks or recommended contractual/technical safeguards

- A short memo addressing the numbered questions above and redlines for
