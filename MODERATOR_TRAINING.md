# Moderator Training: 1-hour session outline

Purpose: Train moderators on PDPL basics, UAE cybercrime/defamation risk,
redaction, evidence handling, and the SOP.

Duration: 60 minutes

Slides / Sections
1) (5 min) Introduction & objectives
- Why moderation matters; project context; legal environment in UAE.

2) (10 min) PDPL fundamentals (high-level)
- What is personal data; lawful bases; data subject rights; retention; cross
  border notes (link to FULL_REPORTING_PRIVACY_NOTICE.md)

3) (10 min) UAE cybercrime & defamation overview (high-level)
- Criminal defamation risk; doxxing and publication of private data; avoid
  public naming; escalate to counsel for high-risk cases.

4) (15 min) Evidence handling & redaction
- Chain of custody; preserving originals; redaction examples (screenshots with
  PII removed); where to store unredacted master files.

5) (10 min) SOP walkthrough & role-play
- Intake to outcome workflow; triage checklist; approval gates; appeal
  process. Quick role-play: intake → triage → outcome.

6) (5 min) Q&A and resources
- Key contacts: enforcement email (almiccy@gmail.com), legal counsel
  contact (TBD), links to PDPL and repo materials.

Materials provided
- Slide deck (I can generate a markdown slide deck or PPTX on request).
- Quick reference card for redaction & triage checklist.
