# Communication Templates (intake / updates / outcomes / appeals)

Use these templated messages for consistent, legally-safe communication.

1) Intake acknowledgement (send within 3 business days)

Subject: Report received — Case ID: {{CaseID}}

Message:
Hello {{ReporterName}},

Thank you for your report to the {{ProjectName}} enforcement team. We have
received your submission and assigned Case ID: {{CaseID}}. We will review the
information and aim to respond within 3 business days with a short status
update.

We treat reports confidentially. If you have immediate safety concerns, please
state them in your reply. Otherwise, we will contact you if we need additional
information.

Regards,
Enforcement Team
{{ContactEmail}}

2) Investigation update (optional)

Subject: Update on Case {{CaseID}} — {{ProjectName}}

Message:
Hello {{ReporterName}},

This is a short update: we have completed triage and are conducting an
investigation. Current status: {{Status}}. We will notify you of the outcome
once the investigation is complete. If you have further evidence, reply to
this email and attach files; avoid posting case materials publicly.

Regards,
Enforcement Team

3) Outcome notification (private)

Subject: Outcome — Case {{CaseID}} — {{ProjectName}}

Message:
Hello {{Name}},

We have completed our review of Case {{CaseID}}. Summary of findings:

- Allegation summary: {{ShortAnonymisedSummary}}
- Evidence relied upon: {{EvidenceSummary}}
- Decision: {{Decision}} (no action / correction / warning / suspension / ban)
- Rationale: {{Rationale}}

If you disagree with this outcome, you may submit an appeal within 14 days by
replying to this email with any new evidence.

Regards,
Enforcement Team

4) Appeal acknowledgement (send on receipt)

Subject: Appeal received — Case {{CaseID}}

Message:
Hello {{Name}},

We have received your appeal for Case {{CaseID}}. A different moderator will
review the appeal and we expect to provide a decision within 14 days.

Regards,
Enforcement Team

5) Public learning summary template (anonymised)

- Case ID: {{CaseID}} (anonymised)
- Issue type: {{IssueType}}
- Key learning: {{AnonymisedLearning}}
- Actions taken: {{AnonymisedActions}}

Note: Remove timestamps, usernames, message quotes that could identify parties.
