# Reporting Privacy Notice

This Reporting Privacy Notice explains how the project collects, uses, stores,
and protects personal data received through conduct reports and related
investigations. It is a summary intended for reporters and respondents; the
project’s full privacy policy and data processing documentation are available
on request.

Authoritative references
- UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021): https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/data/data-protection-laws
- Contributor Covenant FAQ: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/faq/
- GitHub: Adding a code of conduct to your project: https://docs.github.com/en/communities/setting-up-your-project-for-healthy-contributions/adding-a-code-of-conduct-to-your-project

1. Data controller and contact
- Data controller: Repository maintainers (almiccy-source). For reporting,
  contact: almiccy@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect
- Reporter information: name, email, affiliation (if provided).
- Respondent information: name, user handle, and any identification needed to
  investigate.
- Case materials: text of messages, issue/PR references, screenshots, files,
  timestamps, and any attachments submitted by the reporter.
- Metadata: IP addresses, timestamps, and system logs when necessary for
  security or abuse investigation.

3. Purpose & lawful basis
- Purpose: to investigate Code of Conduct reports, take corrective action,
  and maintain community safety and integrity.
- Lawful basis: legitimate interests of the project and compliance with legal
  obligations (PDPL). Where required, we will seek the reporter's consent for
  specific disclosures.

4. Data minimisation
- We only collect what is necessary to investigate and resolve the complaint.
- Reporters should avoid providing irrelevant sensitive personal data.

5. Retention
- Investigation records are retained for up to 24 months from case closure by
  default, unless legal obligations require longer retention. Case summaries may
  be retained in anonymised form for community learning.

6. Access control and security
- Access to raw reports and investigation files is strictly limited to the
  enforcement team and legal counsel as required.
- Stored data is protected using strong access controls and encryption when at
  rest and in transit (depending on storage provider capabilities).

7. Cross-border transfers
- Reports may be stored or processed outside the UAE (for example, on GitHub
  infrastructure or third-party services). Where transfers occur, we will seek
  to ensure appropriate safeguards and compliance steps consistent with the
  PDPL (e.g., documented legal basis or contractual safeguards). Contact the
  enforcement email for details about specific transfers.

8. Data subject rights
- Reporters and respondents may request access to, correction of, or deletion
  of their personal data to the extent permitted by applicable law. Send
  requests to almiccy@gmail.com. Some information may be retained if required
  by law or for legitimate defence against claims.

9. Disclosure to law enforcement and other third parties
- The project will comply with lawful requests from law enforcement and may
  disclose information when required by law (for example, in response to a
  court order or local authority request in the UAE).

10. Automated decision-making
- No automated decision-making based solely on reporting data is used for
  enforcement outcomes.

11. Changes to this notice
- We may update this notice from time to time. The most recent version will be
  maintained in the repository.

12. Complaint to supervisory authority
- If you believe your PDPL rights are violated, you may contact the UAE Data
  Office or the competent supervisory authority. See the PDPL references above
  for official guidance.
